World-Conquer

Build a base, harvest crystal and defeat the computer player. All art, sound and voices are original; the game runs entirely in this tab.

Sound starts after your first click.
How to play

Goal: destroy every enemy building and unit. You lose if all of yours are destroyed.

  1. Press Play, then choose Multiplayer and Skirmish. Pick a map and press OK.
  2. Click your Mobile Base to select it, then click it again to unfold it into a Construction Yard.
  3. Build from the sidebar on the right: click an icon to build it, then click the map to place a structure. Start with a Power Plant and a Refinery.
  4. Add a Barracks and a Vehicle Factory, then train an army and attack.

Controls

  • Select: click a unit, or drag a box around several.
  • Move / attack: with units selected, click the ground or an enemy.
  • Scroll: arrow keys, or move the mouse to the screen edge.
  • Deselect: right-click.
  • Sell / repair: the buttons above the sidebar, then click a building.
  • Options: the tab at the top left (also Esc).
  • Full screen: F12 (again, or Esc, to leave).

Your forces

  • Construction Yard: builds every other structure.
  • Power Plant: powers your base; build more when power runs low.
  • Refinery: turns crystal into credits; comes with a Harvester.
  • Barracks: trains Rifle Squads.
  • Vehicle Factory: builds Scout Cars, Battle Tanks and Harvesters.
  • Guard Tower: a fixed gun post that defends your base.
  • Harvester: gathers crystal and brings it to the Refinery.
  • Rifle Squad: cheap infantry, good against other infantry.
  • Scout Car: fast and lightly armed; good for scouting.
  • Battle Tank: slow, armoured and strong against vehicles and buildings.

Engine diagnostics

Platform checks inside the real engine module. No game data is required. Each probe takes over the canvas; reload to switch modes.

The SDL2 probe animates a rectangle and logs mouse and keyboard input. The cooperative probe runs a synchronous C++ loop that yields to the browser through Asyncify.

The SHP diagnostic loads a project-owned sprite and palette from content/ and draws it with the engine's CC_Draw_Shape(). The wireframe diagnostic does the same for six placeholder shapes from wireframes/.

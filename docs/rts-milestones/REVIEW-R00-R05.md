## Completion-critical follow-ups

1. **Dynamic authoritative world and definitions — R18 (new).** R00's stable contract IDs are fixed `UNIT_IDS`, `CRATE_IDS` and `EXTRACTION_ID`; owner IDs and selection are baked into Courier. `Contract::view()` assumes exactly four infantry, two crates and one extraction, and hard-codes map bounds. Its current sequence duplicate guard checks *pending* commands, not previously executed sequences, while the future multiplayer contract requires replay/epoch rules. Before R02/R03/R04 build broad orders/combat/maps, introduce one dynamic house/entity/definition lifecycle, deterministic allocation/validation, versioning and C++→HUD compatibility. Preserve Courier behavior. These are forward-compatibility tasks, not regressions that invalidate the accepted R00 scope.
2. **Real gameplay art binding — R19 (new).** R05 explicitly records that `r05.*` IDs are not R00 instance IDs and that gallery animation states are not invoked by the native match. Its pack has no weapon stats, unit costs, engine building placement, resource passability or production tech definitions. Once R03/R04/R07/R08/R09 exist, enforce a one-to-one validated mapping to live actor/structure/resource/effect definitions and ensure that the real C++/WASM match, minimap and HUD render the appropriate direction, faction palette, animation and fog state. Without this, a game could technically complete R08/R13 and still ship fallback placeholders while R05 assets only appear in a gallery.
3. **Acceptance and documentation coordination.** R02/R03/R04 now depend on R18, R13 on R19 and R16 explicitly on R19; later work must respect the same accepted entity/art identity split. R00 and R05 are not reopened, and the separately accepted PR/CI evidence remains intact.

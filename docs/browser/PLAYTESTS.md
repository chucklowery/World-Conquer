# Playtests

Player gates (roadmap, "Playtest protocol") are recorded here. A gate is met
only by a session on the **published** site with at least three people who
have not worked on the project, and no help beyond the in-game text and the
play page's guide.

## How to run a session

1. Send each tester the play link: https://chucklowery.github.io/World-Conquer/
   and nothing else. Do not explain the controls or the goal.
2. Ask them to play one skirmish against the computer, thinking aloud if
   they can (a screen recording helps, but is optional).
3. After the match, ask the questions below and fill in one table row per
   tester. Quote them exactly for the "quote" column.

Questions:

- Which browser and operating system did you use?
- How long from opening the link until your match started? (Measure it if
  you are watching.)
- Did you finish a match (win or lose)? If not, why did you stop?
- What could you not identify or work out?
- For each of these, what is it for? Construction Yard, Power Plant,
  Refinery, Barracks, Vehicle Factory, Guard Tower, Harvester, Rifle Squad,
  Scout Car, Battle Tank. (Score how many of the 10 they named correctly.)
- Anything else? (one quote)

## M5 player gate

Pass when: 3 of 3 testers start a match unaided, at least 2 of 3 finish a
match against the AI, and every tester names what each of the 10 roster
objects is for.
